# P3 nutrition and physiology checkpoint

## Setup

- Five paired seeds: 42–46
- 50 P3 physiology-varied founders, 20,000 ticks
- Identical world seed and water configuration per pair
- Treatment changes plant nutrition multiplier: poor `0.5`, rich `1.5`

## Result

| Measure | Poor nutrition | Rich nutrition |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Final population range | 64–170 | 325–476 |
| Birth range | 422–686 | 1,539–2,148 |
| Cumulative plant food range | 7,336–9,224 | 26,854–26,928 |

Rich nutrition consistently produced larger persistent populations, more births, and approximately three times the plant-food energy flow. Temperature tolerance, fertility investment, and lifespan tendency remained genetically varied in both treatments.

## Interpretation and limitation

Plant nutrition is a material P3 environmental pressure. This run does not isolate temperature selection: the current treatment changes nutritional value, not the temperature field itself. It is therefore a P3 ecology/physiology checkpoint, not a completed temperature-adaptation proof.
